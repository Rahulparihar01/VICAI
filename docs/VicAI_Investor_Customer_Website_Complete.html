VicAI does more than display reviews. It creates a controlled process to request feedback, follow up customers, manage responses and show what is happening across every location.

    Automated Google review requestsSend an approved review link after a completed job, appointment, purchase or service milestone by SMS, email or WhatsApp.

    Multi-site reputation analyticsCompare ratings, review volume, response time, themes and trends by location, service, campaign or reporting period.

Common questions

What businesses usually ask first.

Can VicAI work with our current business software?

Yes. Growth includes a standard ServiceM8 connection. Pro includes an allowance for minor custom API maintenance. New or complex integrations are quoted as a separate implementation project.

Does the AI replace our staff?

No. VicAI handles repetitive enquiries, qualification, booking and follow-up. Staff stay in control and can take over any conversation.

Are phone, SMS and WhatsApp costs unlimited?

No. Each tier has agreed allowances. Extra voice minutes and messages are charged through clear overage rates or usage packs.

Can VicAI give medical, legal or financial advice?

The standard platform is for customer engagement and administration. It does not provide unrestricted clinical, legal or financial advice. Any higher-risk use needs a separate scope, controls and governance review.

How long does setup take?

Simple digital setups can be completed quickly once access and content are ready. Voice, multi-site workflows and custom integrations require a planned onboarding and testing process.

How does VicAI manage Google reviews?

VicAI can send approved Google review links after a completed service, follow up customers who have not responded, monitor connected Google Business Profile reviews, create reply drafts, support internal comments and approval, and publish approved replies where account permissions allow.

Can VicAI stop negative reviews?

No platform can guarantee that. VicAI can identify low ratings quickly, alert the right staff, create a customer-recovery task and hold sensitive public replies for approval. The aim is faster response, better service recovery and a clear audit trail.

Can we manage several Google Business Profile locations?

Yes. Pro includes multi-site reputation controls and location-level reporting. Enterprise supports more complex structures such as brands, regions, custom roles, approval rules and reporting requirements.
